PRIMARY GOAL
==================================================

Create a high-oblique bird's-eye reconstruction of Waterford / Veðrafjǫrðr around AD1050.

The image must read first as a river-and-marsh town on triangular ground, then as a dense timber urban settlement.

This is not Dublin copied to Waterford.

==================================================
GEOGRAPHY MUST SHOW
==================================================

1. River Suir must be the dominant water body along the northern / northeastern side of the town.
2. St John's River / associated marsh must constrain the southern / southeastern side as a broad wetland relationship.
3. The settlement must read as occupying triangular ground between the Suir and marshy low ground.
4. Preserve an older eastern Dundory focus while showing substantial westward mid-eleventh-century urban growth.
5. Natural water / marsh must remain visually distinct from defensive earthworks.

DO NOT draw the St John's marsh edge as a crisp surveyed polygon.
DO NOT imply an exact reconstructed AD1050 shoreline.

IMPORTANT:

==================================================

The town must read as urban plots rather than a random village scatter.

Use this organization repeatedly but with variation:

street
→ frontage plot
→ principal timber house
→ side access / path
→ smaller rear structure
→ yard / pit / work area

Appropriate house language:

- irregular frontage alignment

- fences / plot divisions where useful

Roofs may use subdued thatch-like historical presentation language, but this is interpretive rather than a direct roof-material claim.
