# Early Medieval Ireland Explorer

Public showcase site for the **Become the High King / 成为至高王** historical research project.

Live site: https://njzhl93.github.io/early-medieval-ireland-explorer/

Current showcase: **Dublin / Dyflin, c. AD 900–1050**.

The public site separates archaeological evidence, geospatial controls, inferred relationships, and reconstruction geometry. Research-only source geometry and restricted materials remain in the private research repository.

## Featured reconstruction

v0.22 adds a research-informed bird’s-eye reconstruction of **Dyflin / Dublin around AD 950** to the homepage and Dublin deep-dive page.

The image is a **Geometry D presentation layer**. It is not a measured archaeological plan, and its individual houses, vegetation, boats, fields, camera and continuous landscape treatment must not be read as directly measured evidence.

The underlying AD950 constraints remain unchanged: Bank 2, compact post-917 urban mass, natural hydrology distinct from defence, and no continuous water-filled defensive moat.
